Opinions I hold with some conviction. Reasonable people may disagree.

**Tabs over spaces.** Not because I'm right, but because the argument doesn't matter and I've picked a side. The only correct answer is "whatever the linter enforces."

**Dark mode is overrated.** I use it. I'm used to it. But I also read paper books and they don't have a dark mode and that's fine. The argument that dark mode is better for your eyes is not well supported by evidence. It's a preference.

**Most apps have too many features.** The ones people actually love are usually surprisingly constrained. Complexity is easy to add and almost impossible to remove.

**Mechanical keyboards are worth it but not for the reasons people say.** Not because they're faster or more accurate. Because they feel better, which makes you want to use them, which makes the time you spend at a keyboard more pleasant. That's it. That's the whole argument.

**The best camera is the one you have with you** is a cliché but it's also true and I think a lot of photography hobbyists (including past me) spend too much time thinking about gear instead of taking photos.

**Standups that are longer than fifteen minutes are not standups, they're meetings.** Call them meetings. Plan accordingly.

**Most meetings could be emails.** Most emails could be a Slack message. Most Slack messages should be not sent.

**The best time to start a project is before you're ready.** Waiting until conditions are ideal is waiting forever. Done and imperfect ships. Perfect doesn't.
