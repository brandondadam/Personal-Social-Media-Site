I've been meaning to write more for years.

Every week I learn something new, hit a weird bug, or have a half-formed opinion about some tech decision — and then it just evaporates. It lives in a Slack message, a voice memo, or nowhere at all. This is my attempt to stop letting that happen.

## Why a blog and not just social media?

Short posts are fine for quick thoughts, but I kept finding myself wanting more space. Not book-length space — just room to actually walk through a problem, share the context that makes it interesting, and arrive somewhere concrete.

A blog post that takes 5 minutes to read can compress hours of figuring something out. That feels worth doing.

## What I'll write about

Mostly code and the web. Probably some opinions about tools and workflows. Occasionally whatever book or project I'm currently obsessed with.

I'm not planning to stick to a strict niche. The through-line is that everything here is something I actually care about — not content for the sake of content.

## The format

I wanted the reading experience to feel familiar without being generic. Hence the Twitter-style feed — it's a UI I spend a lot of time in, and I like the way it makes you scroll through ideas rather than navigate a list of titles.

Each post shows up as a card in the feed. Click through for the full thing.

Let's see how this goes.
